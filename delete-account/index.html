Delete your YourEden account

YourEden — readysteadygo2006

You can permanently delete your YourEden account and all associated data.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open YourEden
  2. Go to Settings → Manage account
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm

Your account and data are removed immediately.

Option 2 — By email

If you cannot access the app, email home.paul.grant@gmail.com from the address registered to your account, with the subject Delete my account.

We will verify ownership and delete the account within 30 days, and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

  • Your login credentials (email and password)
  • All growing systems and controller records
  • All sensor history, schedules and device configuration
  • All plants, journals and notes
  • Device certificates and cloud device registrations
  • All files stored for your account
  • Any access you have given other people, and invitations you have sent

If you have access to someone else's account as an Admin or Member, deleting your own account ends that access; their account and data are not affected.

What is retained

Nothing is retained after deletion, except anonymised operational logs with no link to you, and any records we are legally required to keep.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
