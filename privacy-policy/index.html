YourEden — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026 Applies to: the YourEden app for Android (com.youreden.app) and iOS

Who we are

YourEden is operated by readysteadygo2006. Privacy contact: home.paul.grant@gmail.com

What we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address and a password. Authentication is handled by AWS Cognito; we never store your password.

Growing system data

When you add a controller, the app and the device send us:

  • Sensor readings (temperature, humidity, water level, nutrient values)
  • Device configuration, schedules and power/pump state
  • System, tray and plant records you create, including journal entries and notes

This data is transmitted from your controller to AWS IoT Core and stored in AWS services (IoT Device Shadow, DynamoDB, S3) in the eu-west-2 (London) region.

Sharing your account with other people

An account owner can invite other people by email to help look after their systems, as an Admin or a Member. To do this we store:

  • the email address you enter for the person you invite
  • the role you give them, who sent the invitation, and when
  • whether the invitation is pending, accepted, declined or withdrawn

We send the invitation from a no-reply address using Amazon SES. The email contains only a link to accept; it expires after 7 days, and expired invitations are deleted automatically.

People who accept can see and control the owner's systems according to their role (some actions, such as adding systems or changing device settings, stay with the owner), and may receive the system's notifications. The owner or an admin can remove someone's access at any time, and anyone can leave an account they were invited to.

Device and technical information

notifications you have enabled

  • Basic app analytics via AWS Pinpoint (app opens, session data)
  • Your controller's hardware identifier (MAC address) and local network address

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your geographic location
  • We do not sell or rent your data
  • We do not share your data with advertisers
  • We do not use third-party tracking or advertising SDKs

Who processes your data

Your data is processed by Amazon Web Services (hosting, authentication, storage, messaging and email delivery) and Google Firebase (push notification delivery only). These providers act on our instructions as data processors.

Support access

If you ask us for help, we may request temporary access to your growing system data to diagnose the problem. This only happens with your explicit consent — you receive a request in the app and must accept it before any access is granted. You can decline, and access is limited to the data in your account (systems, devices, sensor readings, plants and journals) and is used only to resolve your request. We never see your password.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete it as described below.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time:

In the app: Settings → Manage account → Delete Account

On the web: https://readysteadygo2006.github.io/youreden/delete-account

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your login credentials (AWS Cognito)
  • All systems, devices, sensor history, plants and journal entries
  • Your device certificates and cloud device records
  • All files stored for your account
  • Any access you have given other people, and invitations you have sent

If you have access to someone else's account as an Admin or Member, deleting your own account ends that access; their account and data are not affected.

This is immediate and irreversible. We retain no backup copies except where required by law.

Security

Data in transit to our cloud services is encrypted with TLS. Device provisioning over Bluetooth is encrypted at the application layer. Access to your data requires authentication.

Children

YourEden is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We will update the date at the top of this policy when it changes. Material changes will be notified in the app.
